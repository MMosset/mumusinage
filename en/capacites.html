Our Production Capabilities

We enjoy finding solutions for machining work of every kind, from the straightforward to the highly complex. Prototypes and small production runs are our speciality, and we can also set up processes for larger volumes.

Our expertise covers a wide range of machinable materials, including various steels, aluminium alloys, copper and titanium, as well as plastics such as POM, Nylon, Teflon and many others.
