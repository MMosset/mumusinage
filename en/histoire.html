Our Story

The origins and the journey behind Atelier MµM-Usinage

MUM Usinage grew out of a passion for engineering and the ambition to build a workshop on a human scale, one where craftsmanship, precision and a direct relationship with the customer come first.

The workshop took shape step by step, drawing on experience gained in demanding sectors such as watchmaking, medical technology and plastic injection moulding. That breadth built a solid command of machining and a genuinely solution-driven approach.

Guided by a simple ambition - to offer a precision engineering service that is responsive, flexible and accessible - MUM Usinage has made direct contact and quality workmanship its priorities from day one.

Today the workshop continues to grow with the same philosophy: turning customer projects into real, working solutions, from prototype to small production runs, with rigour, precision and passion.

A story that keeps being written, one part at a time.
